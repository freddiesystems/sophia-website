About me

Hi, I'm Sophia.

I'm a student at St John's Durham, studying Primary Education BA with QTS.

See my experience Browse my bookshelf

Through my experience working with children, I have spent hundreds of hours building relationships with students of different ages and abilities. I have thoroughly enjoyed mentoring students and experiencing their confidence grow over the course of our sessions. These experiences have also opened my eyes to the many ways in which children learn. Every child's mind is unique, and I understand that for a child to reach their full potential and meet their academic goals, they need a mentor who will invest the time in understanding their specific learner profile.

An important priority in all my lessons is consolidation. We spend the beginning of every lesson reviewing any new material or areas of difficulty from the previous lesson. This helps my students develop stronger long-term retention of key concepts, rather than relying solely on rote memorisation.

I offer bespoke lessons to keep learning varied and engaging. Once I have got to know a student, I always try to incorporate their interests into our lessons; whether that be using football league tables to practice Maths, percentages and data handling, or creating English EAL activities around Harry Potter. You can see some examples of learning resources I have created for my students in the past here.

One of the comments I receive most often from my students is that my lessons are something they genuinely look forward to, while also noticing an improvement in their academic performance.

I look forward to hopefully hearing from you shortly.

Outside the classroom

A little of me, beyond the lessons

I've always thought children learn most from people who stay curious and active themselves, so here is a little of what I get up to when I'm not teaching.

£750 for a school in Jakarta

After researching the low standard of literacy in impoverished communities in Jakarta, Indonesia, I curated a fundraising meal to provide schools with learning materials and to raise awareness of learning poverty — together we raised £750.

A half marathon

I trained for and ran a half marathon, which taught me as much about patience and quiet persistence as it did about running.

Arts, crafts and knitting

I love making things by hand — I recently knitted myself a jumper — and I bring that same creativity into the activities I plan with the children I look after.

A year abroad & cruise-ship guiding

I spent a year living in Frankfurt, Germany, and I've also worked as a tour guide for cruise ships — I love meeting new people and seeing new places.

Confidentiality & professionalism

Your family's privacy, respected

I will never share the messages we exchange, or any information about your child, with anyone outside our arrangement. I'm committed to protecting the privacy of every family I work with, and to upholding a high standard of professionalism in everything I do.
