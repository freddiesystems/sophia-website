Learning how to approach homework

For many children, learning how to approach homework is a skill in itself — one that needs to be taught and practised. When supporting a child with homework, I am therefore very intentional in how I approach each task. My aim is always to help your child become increasingly independent, rather than reliant on me.

At this stage, I believe critical thinking is one of the most important skills a child can develop. I never make the answer too obvious; instead, I use open-ended questions and subtle prompts to guide their thinking and encourage them to reach the answer themselves. Over time, this positive reinforcement helps children develop the confidence and problem-solving skills to tackle unfamiliar tasks independently.
