How my tutoring works

  1. 1

    Trial session

    Your child shows me what they're struggling with, and we work through it. £15 for the hour — no pressure to continue.

  2. 2

    Find the gaps

    We sit a past paper and take notes on every area they get wrong, so we know exactly what to target.

  3. 3

    A plan, just for them

    I build a structured plan around their weaknesses — many question types, real stretch, and explaining answers out loud.

  4. 4

    You stay in the loop

    After every session you get a short report: what we covered, what went well, and whether we'll revisit it. Usually they finish feeling great.

About me & how I teach

Hi, I'm Sophia.

I'm 19, based at Durham University studying primary education, and I've been working with children for years — as an au pair in Frankfurt, on teaching placements in Spain and Germany, at children's camps, in a primary school classroom, and as a tutor and babysitter.

"You can't make general judgements about a child until you've really spent time with them. So that's where I always start — with who they are."

My teaching philosophy is simple: children learn best when they feel safe, seen and a little bit stretched. I make abstract ideas concrete through play, I get children explaining their thinking out loud so it actually sticks, and I believe — genuinely — that the right support at the right moment can change the whole shape of a child's education. I never give up on the ones who've been told they're "behind".

— Sophia
