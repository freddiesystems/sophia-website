Tutoring for children who learn differently

Talk to me about tutoring

Working with children who have specific learning difficulties (SpLD) taught me to adapt the way I explain things, the pace we move at, and the tools we use — so that a lesson works with the way your child's mind naturally does, rather than against it. There is no single right way to learn, and for children with additional needs that truth matters all the more.

When a child has struggled with learning in the past, it often takes a quiet toll on their confidence. We address that naturally, by pitching each lesson at exactly the right level of challenge, so confidence grows hand in hand with their progress.

How I adapt

Every plan starts by understanding your child properly — their strengths, their interests, and where the sticking points really are. From there we build something that belongs to them, and we adjust it as we go.
